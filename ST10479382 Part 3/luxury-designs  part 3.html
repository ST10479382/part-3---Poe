Our Story

Luxury Designs was created for fashion-conscious men and women aged 20–45 who want to look sharp without paying full designer prices.

We believe high-end style should be accessible. Every piece is carefully chosen for quality, fit and contemporary appeal.

All orders are shipped nationwide from our Johannesburg warehouse.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you ship nationwide? +
Yes. We ship to every province in South Africa from our Johannesburg warehouse. Delivery usually takes 2–5 business days.
What is your return policy? +
You may return unworn items within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in original condition with tags attached.
Are the products authentic quality? +
Absolutely. We source premium fabrics and work with trusted manufacturers to deliver designer-level quality at accessible prices.
How can I track my order? +
Once your order is dispatched you will receive an email with a tracking number and link.
